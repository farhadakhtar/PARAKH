## What was deliberately not done

**The ground-truth ledger was not used as labels.** It records injected *data-quality* noise - `missing:vendor_name`, `placeholder:date_approval`, `date_order:approval_before_proposal`. None of those is an overpayment, a ghost beneficiary or a procurement irregularity, so mapping them onto audit categories would be fabrication. The ledger's own note also states: *"NEVER join this into the corpus before Stage 7 evaluation - it leaks labels."*

**No rows were invented to reach the 200-label target.** With zero audit documents the honest count is zero.

## To make this dataset real

Place genuine audit PDFs (CAG, PSU, SEAR) in `Data/` and re-run. The parser in step 3 is implemented and functional - it extracts issue type, severity, geography and rupee amounts, and handles crore/lakh units. It has simply had nothing to read.

Even then, treat the output as **weak** labels: they are matched by state, district and year, not by record. PARAKH's Stage 8 requires `provenance='real'` and will refuse anything else.
